## Simple Answers for Sales Leaders

### What is Levvo?
Levvo is the **daily oversight workspace for business owners and sales leaders**. It connects your team's work WhatsApp lines into a single clear dashboard so every lead gets answered fast, assigned to the right rep, and tracked until closed.

### How does Levvo keep my customer data in my hands?
All customer chats, contacts, internal notes, and deal history stay 100% in your company's control. If a sales rep leaves your company or changes handsets, your customer relationships and history stay safely with your business—never on the rep's personal phone.

### Do I get help setting up my team?
Yes! You don't need tech skills or an IT department. Our team provides **direct onboarding support** to walk you through connecting your WhatsApp lines in 5 minutes, configuring lead assignment, and getting your sales floor running smoothly.

### How is Levvo different from traditional CRMs?
Traditional CRMs force sales reps to fill out endless forms that reps hate and managers have to audit. Levvo works directly on your team's live WhatsApp conversations:
* ❌ **NOT a complicated CRM** requiring manual form updates.
* ❌ **NOT a confusing chatbot builder** with complex flowcharts.
* ❌ **NOT a mass spam tool**.
* ✅ **A clean sales cockpit** that helps reps reply faster, distributes leads automatically, and gives leadership full visibility in real time.

## Line Supervision & Team Management

### How do I connect my company WhatsApp numbers?
Connecting your lines takes 5 minutes by scanning a QR code (WhatsApp Web). You can also integrate Meta Cloud API, Telegram, and Web Live Chat if needed.

### How does automatic lead distribution work?
Inbound chats are categorized automatically by topic and routed to available sales reps so every lead is claimed instantly.

### How do chat statuses work?
Keep your inbox organized with three clean statuses:
* **`open`**: Active conversations needing follow-up.
* **`closed`**: Finished chats that automatically reopen if the customer texts back.
* **`blocked`**: Silently filters spam out of your team inbox.

### Can my team use Levvo on desktop and mobile?
Yes! You and your team can access Levvo via:

## Smart Sales Tools & Simple Reporting

### What is AI Deal Assistant?
It's an **on-demand AI assistant for sales reps and managers**. Click it on any chat thread to instantly check if a prospect is ready to buy (`Ready`, `Considering`, `Blocked`), spot deal risks, and get suggested next steps to keep sales moving.

### What are Team Performance Analytics?
Get clear executive summaries showing how fast reps respond, who is following up, and common deal blockers—all generated automatically with zero manual form filling.

### What internal team tools are included?
* **Private Notes:** Add internal notes inside any chat thread visible only to your team.
* **Team Voice Rooms:** Internal audio rooms to talk directly with your reps in real time.
* **Built-in Video Recorder:** Record Loom-style video demos or WhatsApp-style camera videos to send to prospects.
