## How Pricing Works (Made Simple)

*   **Start Free with Welcome Credits:** Sign up in 1 minute without a credit card. Every new workspace comes with initial welcome credits so you can connect your lines and test the platform with your team right away.
*   **Prepaid Credits (Pay as You Go):** Your workspace uses credits based on active reps ($0.27/day per active sales rep, completely free for managers). Top up whenever you want with zero long-term contracts.
*   **Clear Account Dashboard:** Your credit balance is always visible in your account dashboard. If credits run out, feature access steps down gracefully instead of cutting off live chats.
*   **$0 Meta Per-Message Fees:** Connect your company WhatsApp lines directly via QR code. **You pay zero per-message fees for templates, marketing messages, or chat windows.**
